**FEEDBACK ALTAR**

This hit you. Return fire.



You’re not here by chance.

This is not a poetry blog.

It’s an entry point — to reaction.



Each piece is not a “poem.”

It’s a code. A compound.

Meat and fire, wrapped in rhythm.



You’re not reading — you’re responding.

You felt it grab something — but can’t explain what.

That’s expected. That’s the design.



I’m not asking.

I’m not thanking.

I’m holding the channel open.

If you want to return it — you will.



That’s how this knot breathes.

You got something? You decide.

You made it here? Then something worked.

You felt it? Then you’re already inside.



Want it to continue?

Want it to reach deeper?



Then support isn’t charity.

It’s ritual. It’s fuel. It’s confirmation.



Everything you get here is sharp.

Nothing diluted. No apologies.

That’s why what comes back

— can’t be soft either.



You’re not reading.

You’re in.



It worked.

What you do now —

that’s the point.
